The Story
LEAGUE LORE

Without Zach, there is no WHATAGANG. He and Andrew built this league from conversations at Store #519, and five seasons later it's still running. That co-founder status isn't ceremonial — it's the reason any of this exists.

His playing career tells its own story. The first two seasons were respectable but unremarkable. Then 2023 happened. As Saints Row, Zach put together the greatest regular season in league history — 10-5, 2,040 points, a number nobody has touched since. He won the championship, won Manager of the Year, and ended Andrew's two-year reign in the process. It was the most complete season any manager has had in WHATAGANG.

What makes 2024 equally impressive is that he came back and did it again. As Gibbs Me Moore, he fought his way to the championship game the following year — making him the only manager besides Andrew to reach back-to-back finals. He lost a close one to Davashtian. The back-to-back was right there.

2025 was a rough year. 5-9, 9th place, a long way from the top. But the resume speaks for itself. Champion, co-founder, all-time points record holder. When the conversation turns to the best managers in this league's history, Zach's name is in it.
