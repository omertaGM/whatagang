League History & Legends
LEAGUE LORE
05
🍔
The Origin Story
WHATAGANG was born at Whataburger Store #519. A group of coworkers turned a shift job into a fantasy football dynasty. The league name, the logo, the culture — all of it traces back to one burger joint. Est. 2021.
👑
The Back-to-Back
Andrew (Buck-ees) is the only manager in WHATAGANG history to win back-to-back championships. 2021 as Najee's in Paris, 2022 as Long Live Chubbs. Nobody else has come close. The standard.
👻
Anderson Hurley Huggins III
A founding member. A legend. A ghost. Anderson Hurley Huggins III played 2021 and 2022 as Lil Sniqquhs, finished 3rd in the inaugural season, and was never seen again. His name lives forever in these halls.
🏃
Domaniq's Great Escape
Domaniq (StroudTown) played 4 seasons without a playoff appearance, setting the all-time drought record at 5 years. In 2024 he finished last, faced the Milk Mile punishment, and chose to leave the league instead. Replaced by Josh in 2025.
🚽
The Toilet Bowl Punishment
Last place pays the price. The punishment tradition started around 2023. 2024: Domaniq — Milk Mile. He left instead. 2025: Andrew — 24 hours at Whataburger. He actually did it. The first manager to ever serve the sentence. A true WHATAGANG soldier. 🍔
📈
The Comeback Kid — 2025
Josh (Joshua's Deepball) started the 2025 season 1-5 and looked dead in the water. He finished 8-6, made the playoffs, and won the whole thing. First season in the league. Won it all. Instant legend status.
