The Story
LEAGUE LORE

Adrian's 2022 season was one of the best regular season performances in WHATAGANG history. As JJETTAS, he went 11-4 — tied for the all-time best record — and had Justin Jefferson winning Rookie of the Year on his roster. He made the playoffs and went home in the first round. The best record in the league, and he didn't win. That's the kind of thing that follows a manager around.

He named his 2023 team I Lose Too Much. Self-aware. Not wrong. But 2024 brought a bounce-back — Comeback Player of the Year, a 9-5 record, competing again. And 2025 delivered his best result to date: 3rd place as Indiana Jones.

The foundation is there. Four seasons, consistently in the mix, best regular season record in league history. The first championship is still ahead of him, but the track record says it's coming.
