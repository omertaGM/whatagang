The Story
LEAGUE LORE

Three times to the championship game. Three times he's watched someone else hoist it. Jeremiah is the most decorated finalist in WHATAGANG history and the only manager who has never won the whole thing despite consistently being one of the last ones standing.

He won Manager of the Year in the inaugural 2021 season, went 9-6, made the final — and lost to Andrew. He came back in 2022, made it to 3rd. Then 2023 happened. Double Kupp. A 2-13 record that still stands as the worst single season performance in league history. From finalist to the basement in one year.

He recovered. Made the 2025 championship game as Super Lamario, only to run into Josh's historic rookie run. Three finals appearances, zero rings. The question isn't whether Jeremiah can get there — he's proven he can. The question is whether 2026 is finally the year he closes it out.
