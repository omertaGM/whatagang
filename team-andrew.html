The Story
LEAGUE LORE

Andrew is the standard. Two championships in the first two seasons of WHATAGANG — a back-to-back that no other manager has come close to replicating. He won the inaugural title in 2021 with a losing record, which set the tone for everything this league became. You don't have to be the best team. You just have to be the best team when it matters.

2022 was the confirmation. As Long Live Chubbs, he ran it back and became the only manager in WHATAGANG history to win consecutive championships. Manager of the Year. Back-to-back champion. The case was closed.

The years since have been quieter. Missed the playoffs in 2023. Made them in 2024 and went home early. Then 2025 happened — INJUR-EES, last place, and a date with destiny. 24 hours at Whataburger Store #519. The place where it all started. He sat there. He served it. Every other manager who faced the punishment found a reason to leave or dodge it. Andrew walked in.

The commissioner. The back-to-back champion. The first man to ever serve the sentence. The standard hasn't changed — just the circumstances.
