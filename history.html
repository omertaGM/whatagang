In The Beginning — Store #519
It started at a Whataburger. Not a bar, not a fantasy football draft party, not a group chat. A Whataburger. Specifically Store #519, where a group of coworkers spent more time talking trash about NFL rosters than taking orders. Someone said let's make a league. Nobody said no. WHATAGANG was born in 2021 with 6 teams, no real rules, and zero expectations. Five seasons later it's still running. The name stayed. The logo pays homage. The punishments got worse. Some members left. Legends were made. This is the story.
Season 1 · The Inaugural Year
SEASON2021
🏆
Champion

Najee's in Paris
The first ever WHATAGANG season. 6 teams, Store #519 energy, and zero idea what this would become. Andrew won the whole thing with a 7-8 losing record — somehow making the playoffs and running the table. Pure chaos. Jeremiah finished runner-up. Anderson Hurley Huggins III — a founding member whose real name sounds like a Supreme Court justice — finished 3rd as Lil Sniqquhs. It was the last time anyone would see him. A Manager-less bot finished last. Nobody had to do any punishment. Nobody even knew there would ever be a punishment.

Season 2 · The Back-to-Back
SEASON2022
🏆
Champion 👑 Back-to-Back

The league expanded to 10 teams. New faces, new chaos. Andrew somehow did it again as Long Live Chubbs, becoming the only back-to-back champion in WHATAGANG history. Adrian's JJETTAS had the best record in the league at 11-4 and still didn't win it. Anderson Hurley Huggins III returned for one final season as Lil Sniqquhs, finishing last with a 4-11 record. It was his last appearance in the league. Rookie of the Year went to Justin Jefferson on Adrian's roster. The Wooden Ship award was not yet established but the seeds were planted.

SEASON2025

Champion

Joshua's Deepball
Josh replaced Domaniq and immediately made everyone wish they'd taken him more seriously. He started 1-5. He looked done. The first manager in WHATAGANG history to actually do the punishment. A soldier.
